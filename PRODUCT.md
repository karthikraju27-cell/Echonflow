# Echonflow
## Platform
Responsive web app: Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind, Supabase, Vercel.
## Product vision
One account-first platform with three layers: Individuals (ERA, learning and certification), Providers (enrollment, listings, WRS and inquiries), and Vārtā (insights and future advertising/affiliate discovery).
Company pilots support the individual journey. Reporting and CRM are supporting tools.
Preserve existing learning content, scoring, certificates, provider CRUD and open Vārtā submissions.
Personal ERA results must never appear on public seeker profiles. WRS scoring remains explicitly provisional.
## Open work
Advertising inventory and affiliate recommendation implementation remain separate from this visual update. Never fabricate offers, testimonials, or scores.
